# UrbanFleet Operations — Vehicle Service Management Application

## US-004: Approve Service Estimate

### Overview
This user story introduces an **Approval** stage into the case lifecycle. It allows the Customer persona to review the service estimate generated by the Service Advisor (US-003) and formally approve or reject it, determining whether the case proceeds to service execution or is closed out.

---

### Case Type: Vehicle Service Request

**Stage:** `Approval`
- Follows the `Inspection` stage (US-002/US-003), where inspection findings and the cost estimate (Labor Cost, Parts Cost, Total Cost) have already been recorded.
- Represents the point where the Customer decides whether to proceed with the proposed service.

---

### Step Configuration

**Step Name:** Approve Service Estimate
**Persona:** Customer

| Property         | Type              | Description                                             | Required |
|--------------------|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Approval Status    | Dropdown/Pick List | Customer's decision on the estimate (Approved / Rejected) | Yes      |

The Customer reviews the earlier-captured Inspection Notes, Condition Rating, and Total Cost before making a decision, ensuring the approval is informed.

---

### Process Flow (Routing Logic)

Based on the value of **Approval Status**, the case routes down one of two paths:

- **Approved** → Case moves forward to the **Service Execution** stage (assignment, repair, etc.).
- **Rejected** → Case is routed to a resolution path and closed with an appropriate status (e.g., "Resolved-Rejected" or "Closed-Customer Declined"), without proceeding to repair work.

This branching ensures the case never advances to service execution without explicit customer approval.

---

### Persona Access
- **Customer**: Views the estimate (Total Cost) and inspection summary; selects Approval Status (Approved/Rejected).
- **Service Advisor**: No edit access at this stage — awaits the customer's decision.

---

### Sample Test Case

**Approval Decision (following US-003 estimate):**
- Vehicle ID: UF-2024-0157
- Total Cost: ₹4,000
- Approval Status: Approved

**Expected Outcome:**
1. Customer views the inspection summary and Total Cost (₹4,000).
2. Customer selects "Approved" as the Approval Status.
3. Case transitions out of the Approval stage into Service Execution.
4. If Approval Status had been "Rejected" instead, the case would route to a resolution/closure path with status reflecting the customer's decline, and no repair work would be scheduled.
